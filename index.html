📜 Storyline Overview

In Shadow Fight: Special Edition, you play as Shadow, a legendary fighter who loses his physical body after opening the Gates of Shadows. Your mission is to defeat powerful demons such as Lynx, Hermit, Butcher, and Titan to restore balance to the world and regain your form. The enhanced storyline reveals new chapters of Shadow’s past, offering players deeper insight into the Shadow universe.
